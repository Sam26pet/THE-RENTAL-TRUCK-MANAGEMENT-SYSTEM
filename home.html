About Us

The Rental Truck Management System Foundation was established to modernize logistics and transport services in Tanzania. Our mission is to provide a unified digital platform that connects customers, truck owners, and service centers in one transparent and efficient ecosystem.

We operate by integrating mini trucks, guta (three-wheelers), refrigerated vans, town service vehicles, and larger trucks into a single system. Customers can easily register, book vehicles, make secure payments, and track deliveries in real-time. Service providers benefit from automated contracts, maintenance records, and analytics dashboards that improve accountability and reduce costs.

Why Choose Us?

  • Transparency and convenience for customers
  • Analytics and maintenance records for providers
  • Secure contracts and GPS tracking
  • Loyalty programs to reward repeat customers
